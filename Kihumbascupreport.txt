                             THE REWARD SYSTEM   
 In the beginning, I used a participation reward system to award points. Now, basically, with this system, I am spending time
 and effort to record game data, sanitize it and present it in an easy to understand way. This is information and
 with information, comes responsibility of knowing who you share which information with. participation information tends to
 share information about how much time a player spends in vintage in a direct manner(days and games played) which people would not really 
 appreciate  .
 Also the participation reward system rewards participation and not winning so a player will have more points just by playing more games even if they
 don't win as much. So I had to change it to a winning reward system. At first, I thought of having two tables, one which I called
 participation table and the other one I called a leaderboard. But I just added some fields to hold win data. (Win Points WP 
 and Games won GW). but using code I am able to only show the fields that made up the leaderboard.
